# Prospective not-surfaced review

Open **Not-surfaced review** from a dated prospective evaluation, or visit `/retrospective/<morning-id>`. The page uses that morning's immutable eligible universe and latest append-only outcome revisions. Today's broker catalogue, tracking and purchase state do not affect membership. No history before saved operation is reconstructed.

The top ten rank resolved H20 returns after the same 10bp-per-side costs used in prospective evaluation; equal returns sort by stable security identity. H10/H30 support the primary horizon with complete-denominator coverage and each leader's supporting results. Pending, unresolved and awaiting-calculation members remain counted. Daily-close downside retains complete/partial coverage and does not describe intraday risk. Returns use hypothetical official-opening references, not actual fills.

Flags can overlap: AI-main, baseline-only, early watch, early-watch-only (neither main nor baseline), researched/rejected, outside research cap, and momentum-gate failure. Other research outcomes and explicit gaps remain visible. **Not surfaced** means absent from morning AI main; it does not establish detectability. Refresh publications are linked separately and never rewrite morning flags.

Historical context uses the morning's saved decision/version IDs, screening input IDs and cited evidence IDs. It does not follow corrected assessments or search for later explanations. Evidence acquisition and publication timestamps are checked against the morning cutoff. Later evidence, if referenced, is labeled hindsight and excluded from historical detectability; missing retained IDs are explicit. Unknown publication time supports availability only, not event timing. Outcome corrections can change ranking; their IDs, acquisition cutoffs and recorded times link to immutable calculation history.

The separate coverage ledger lists saved eligibility exclusions and screening/history gaps, grouped by reason and paginated in 100-entry ticker order with `coverageAfter`. Historically excluded securities are not counted as misses. An eligible member with a screening gap still retains its outcome denominator. The page shows the complete H10/H20/H30 outcome denominators using 100-member reads and retains only ten ranked members between pages; historical context is fetched only for those leaders. Runtime grows with the saved universe. Local scale verification does not establish hosted combined-workload capacity.

Small samples, overlapping cohorts, incomplete data and later revisions limit interpretation. The page presents retrospective results, never a historical prediction or evidence of statistical selection edge. Existing live data, private access, budget, backup and deployment gates remain in force. No deployment or source/model calls are required to read saved results.
