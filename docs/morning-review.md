# Morning review

Issue 08 provides the local morning report loop and a private server-rendered dashboard. It does not establish deployed uptime or investment usefulness.

Five-minute UTC ticks calculate Zagreb due times with IANA zones and the saved US session calendar. Morning acquisition begins at 06:30, new history requests close at 07:45, research closes at 07:50, publication targets 07:55 and the deadline is 08:00. A known exchange holiday skips morning creation. Without a saved calendar, bootstrap must establish the session; the browser labels session knowledge unavailable. Collections and recovery continue independently of holidays. At or after 08:00 a tick records an overdue event if a known session has no committed report; a browser visit detects the same absence without waiting for Cron.

The existing coordinator checkpoints capture → frozen market universe → screening → bounded research → evaluation collection planning → morning publication. Successful checkpoints reset attempt counts. Failed stages retry with the existing backoff and terminate after three counted failures; provider retries retain their own ledger. Pending history is closed explicitly at the acquisition margin. An already frozen market snapshot resumes qualification idempotently. Seed and collection jobs do not publish discovery reports.

Publication requires committed screening, research and early-watch artifacts and opening-reference collection planned for every saved eligible security. Issue 05's evaluation planner also schedules raw endpoint/action collection and the continuing collector maintains H30 observations, including removed members. The exact U_D and its input IDs remain in immutable `market_decisions` under the frozen snapshot; the report stores that snapshot reference, count and screening input hash rather than duplicating all historical daily data. Both cohort arms use this same hash and information cutoff. The report, committed assessment references, dispositions, novelty, early-watch history reference and AI/baseline membership commit in one fenced D1 batch. `(report_date, rule_version)` is unique. No rejected/unresearched candidate is backfilled.

A separate primary-session readback verifies the report hash, exact cohort memberships/input hashes/cutoffs, saved universe and referenced assessment versions. Only after these reads complete does the server sample the observation clock and append a receipt. A valid receipt observed by 08:00 qualifies timing; missing receipt is unverified and recovery after 08:00 is late/research-only. Neither transaction-start time nor a previous source timestamp can certify earlier availability. Existing receipts and memberships cannot be rewritten on replay. Downstream evaluation must use valid matching receipts, never just membership rows.

The private `/` dashboard labels pending, overdue, exchange-closed and calendar-unavailable states, retains a previous dated report, and exposes failures, retries and budgets. `/morning/:id` offers up to three candidates, up to five early-watch entries, baseline and all research dispositions. Cited facts, interpretations, unknowns, conditional thesis, invalidation, market/feed/acquisition times and expiry are visible. Original source URLs and immutable assessments remain navigable. Dated provider symbols and broker tickers identify frozen main/watch members; account availability remains explicitly unverified pending investor confirmation in Trading 212. Repeat cards link prior assessments and identify changed content fields; first main appearances after early watch are promoted/new. Historical SIP prices never validate a live entry. Current main assessments offer manual tracking controls. The report includes immutable daily tracked checks; [the tracking runbook](tracking.md) describes capacity, explicit archive/renewal, invalidation and history.

Review feedback uses same-origin checks, signed CSRF and bounded bodies for both HTML forms and JSON. Responses are optional and append-only, with explicit no-response and idempotent command IDs. The latest submission per report controls monthly response counts; old submissions remain auditable. Eligible denominators include dated published complete/partial/empty reports, exclude unavailable reports, and do not depend on returns or timing qualification. Minutes/usefulness have their own answered denominators; explicit no-response and missing feedback are separate. Dashboard feedback shows median minutes, proportion within 15 minutes and usefulness distribution, independently of outcomes.

## Verification and remaining gates

Local tests exercise screening through cited assessment/report publication, private reviewer navigation, missing research, baseline preservation, interrupted report/cohort transactions, post-deadline recovery, replay, feedback/no-response and HTML forms, exchange holidays and the EU/US autumn DST mismatch. The source/model responses in the representative run are permitted synthetic fixtures; no billed provider or model call is needed. Escaping and the existing Access JWT/CSRF policy cover every new route, including stylesheet, sources, assessments and forms.

No synthetic run measures a human's review time or proves remote availability. Before routine operation, complete the existing operations, market-capture and research runbook gates: account/model availability and live fixture quality; identity/action coverage; combined full-universe hosted CPU/heap/query/latency and completion by 07:50; actual Access/origin configuration; independent encrypted backup/key custody; verified total recurring costs; and three observed pre-08:00 morning sessions. Record human review minutes and usefulness prospectively. Jobs and research remain disabled by default. No deployment, paid resource or provider outreach is implied.
