# Market capture

Issue [05](../.scratch/investment-intelligence-mvp/issues/05-market-snapshots.md) implements the `screen-eval-v1` market boundary. This is local capture software, not a deployed morning report. Deterministic screening/ranking is documented in [screening](screening.md); research and publication of selections belong to later tickets. Keep both job switches disabled until the operational gates below are satisfied.

## Data and replay

`MarketCapture` accepts dated catalogues, a bounded exchange calendar, reconciled identities, and provider pages. Daily decisions retain every broker row, including structural exclusions and unresolved inputs. US membership joins USD STOCK records to NYSE/NASDAQ working schedules; ticker suffixes are never a filter or identity rule. Unsupported company-share types are excluded explicitly. Catalogue presence is not account order availability.

Normalized raw/split bars, opening events and actions are content-addressed and immutable. A changed observation appends a version linked to its prior logical observation. Query-specific as-of symbol, feed, basis, mapping/action version and acquisition provenance live in immutable page receipts. Identical economic observations reuse their IDs across queries and report days. Indexed sightings retain original acquisition times when history is reused; `reused_at` identifies the later use. A changed split action version invalidates reuse of the older adjusted history, while raw history can be shared.

Frozen manifests reference the exact inputs used. Decisions and manifests cannot be updated/deleted after freezing. Collection of raw outcome observations and reference revisions can continue without changing those decisions. Reads of `/api/market/snapshots/:id?offset=0` require the existing private Access identity and return at most 100 decisions plus whole-manifest coverage counts. `MarketCapture.observations` also paginates at 500. No observation can be newly labeled as acquired in the past.

The data-ready universe requires all 61 expected sessions, valid positive OHLC/volume and consistent bounds on both bases, verified action comparability, a raw close of at least $5, and median raw close × volume of at least $5m over 20 sessions. Eligibility comparisons use decimal arithmetic with the contract’s 12-place half-even rounding. Momentum ratios/logarithms and the frozen baseline are implemented by [issue 06](screening.md). Missing expected bars, unresolved identities/actions and failed required requests remain coverage reasons. A run with gaps is partial, or unavailable when no member can qualify. Structural exclusions and below-price/liquidity decisions are distinct from acquisition failures.

## Source adapters and identity review

Fixed endpoint allowlists permit only Alpaca bars, auctions, corporate actions, paper assets/calendar, Trading 212 instruments/exchanges, and public OpenFIGI ISIN mapping. No account, holdings, order or trading endpoint exists. HTTP redirects are rejected. Alpaca historical bars/auctions always specify SIP and dated `asof`; screening uses `adjustment=split`, outcomes `raw`. Numeric JSON lexemes are retained as decimal strings instead of being rounded through JavaScript Number. Unsupported numeric encodings fail schema validation.

Broker catalogues, provider assets and calendars are refreshed per report and stored in bounded source pages. Every in-scope ISIN receives a listed-only US mapping; no-matches also retain a separate unlisted retry. Positive or negative identity results are evidence, not proof of a current listing. Mapping responses may be reused for up to seven days with their original acquisition time; broker availability and provider asset status are refreshed each report. Cached mappings do not extend a listing review's effective interval.

A FIGI/symbol join does not establish issuer identity, listing continuity or action comparability. The `market_identity_reviews` append-only table supplies dated reconciliation evidence. Its JSON `value` follows `identitySchema` in `src/market/types.ts`: broker ticker, ISIN, stable security/issuer IDs, composite/share-class FIGIs, dated provider symbol, primary venue, security type, listing status, evidence URLs, action version, comparability, and verified action IDs. Each row also has `valid_from`, `valid_through` and actual `acquired_at`. Import reviewed records using a local D1 SQL file; generate a fresh row ID for corrections. Never import the old audit as automatically verified current identities. The audit lacks complete issuer/action reconciliation.

The resolver checks effective dates, FIGI/type agreement and contradictory current asset metadata. An independently reviewed identity can work without a provider trading asset; an inactive/contradictory asset cannot silently pass. Multiple mappings and unresolved listing/type/issuer evidence stay quarantined. Current observations of splits, stock distributions, mergers or other non-cash actions require their provider IDs in the dated verified-action record before qualification. Incomplete consideration, successors, OTC continuation and terminal outcomes stay unresolved; a delisting never implies zero wealth. Outcome arithmetic remains ticket 11.

## Scheduling and collection

The runtime has separate durable bootstrap, identity resolution, history planning, paginated acquisition, qualification and evaluation-planning phases. Each committed page has a fingerprint and next token. A crash after page persistence resumes that checkpoint before another HTTP call. At most three dispatched attempts are allowed per logical page; 429 respects Retry-After. Permission/schema failures are terminal. Invalid multi-symbol requests split into bounded child requests, retaining the original failure; a bad symbol cannot indefinitely poison a whole batch. Local rate/budget deferrals do not consume the provider retry allowance. Successful pages missing the expected session or a qualifying opening schedule at most two additional missing-symbol freshness passes before the deadline. Their immutable earlier receipts remain available; a restart cannot lose the coverage retry after completing the transport page.

The existing singleton renewable/fenced lease protects writes. Invocation work stops before 240 seconds, uses a conservative SQL-query reserve, and bounds individual fetches to 20 seconds. Market pages contain at most 500 observations and 256 KiB of normalized input; catalogue/asset decoded responses are capped at 16 MiB, market responses at 2 MiB, other metadata at 256 KiB–2 MiB. Catalogue and decision SQL inserts are partitioned. Hosted CPU/heap/query bounds still require a real scheduled workload measurement; a synthetic local benchmark cannot certify them.

IANA `America/New_York` exchange opens/closes and `Europe/Zagreb` 08:00 deadlines determine the schedule, including DST mismatch, holidays and early closes. Calendars must cover the previous session, report session and next report deadline; operational acquisition requests 150 prior and 75 future calendar days. No holiday/weekend report is produced. The expected session is independent of the latest returned bar. Cron enqueues morning acquisition from 06:30 Zagreb; late acquisition remains research-only. A missing calendar/catalogue produces an unavailable run rather than a reused current-looking report.

From each saved U_D, official-opening collection is planned after regular open +45 minutes ET, through 08:00 Zagreb on the next exchange-report day. A reference requires primary venue NYSE=N, NASDAQ=Q, AMEX/NYSE American=A, BATS/Cboe BZX=Z or TXSE=F, condition Q, and an event within open…open+30 minutes. Off-primary events, O trades, minute/daily opens and late auctions never substitute. Equal-price duplicates retain provenance; conflicting qualifying prices are unresolved. Initial references freeze by the declared deadline; `freezeReference(...,'revision')` appends later evidence independently.

Daily raw-close/action collection unions saved eligible members through holding session 30. A stock removed from today's catalogue remains in the union and its denominator. Stable identities deduplicate overlapping saved universes; unknown renames/successors remain gaps pending dated review. Current verified mappings take precedence; old dated as-of mappings remain available for removed members. After an outage, missing contiguous holding-session ranges are recovered, including a removed member whose H30 has already matured. Every recovered session retains its original next-report cutoff in `initialDeadlines`; late acquisitions are revision inputs and cannot qualify the initial result. Initial requests retain their next-report deadline. Later corrections must append observations/reference revisions, never replace an initial cohort or its inputs. H10/H20/H30 return calculations are not implemented here.

## Local operation

1. Run `npm run db:migrate`. Migrations 0002–0003 add capture tables and successor evidence without changing foundation artifacts. Back up before a real migration; rollback application code with jobs disabled, retaining immutable data and the additive schema.
2. Supply server bindings `ALPACA_KEY`, `ALPACA_SECRET`, and `TRADING212_AUTHORIZATION` (the exact authorization header value). Never place them in browser code, SQL, exports or logs. Existing Access/CSRF configuration remains mandatory.
3. Configure `budget_limits` for the current UTC day and month, using resources `market_requests`, `broker_instruments`, `broker_exchanges`, and `figi`. Provision rows for every intended UTC day/month, for example a reviewed month at once; missing future periods stop dispatch rather than silently creating new allowances. Missing limits prevent dispatch. Never reset reservations on retry. Requests reserve before dispatch, and unknown responses remain counted.
4. Enforce daily market ceilings ≤600 routine or ≤1,000 for an explicitly requested initial seed. Provider buckets persist across invocations: Alpaca ≤150/minute; OpenFIGI ≤25/minute with ten ISIN jobs/request; Trading 212 instruments ≤1/minute, exchanges ≤2/minute with 30-second spacing. Set documented monthly ceilings and lower operational limits as desired. No paid-data upgrade is automatic.
5. Import current reviewed identity evidence. Until then an honest run may have no data-ready universe. Verify current account terms/permissions and actual source limits before live use.
6. In a local/private deployment, enable `JOBS_ENABLED` and `MARKET_ENABLED`. The default remains disabled. An authenticated same-origin CSRF-protected `POST /api/market/runs` accepts `{ "kind":"market", "reportDate":"YYYY-MM-DD", "seed":true }`. A seed is explicit and may span billing days; it remains late research if its original report deadline passes. A routine request uses `seed:false` and shares the scheduled day's idempotency key. Manual requests wait for Cron; no detached HTTP work runs.
7. Inspect `/api/status`, the private snapshot API, `market_tasks`, source acquisition timestamps, reservations and `market_attempt_events`. Resume pending pages after repair; failed identity/data inputs need a reviewed correction/new version. Do not relabel a historical correction as an on-time selection. Disable both switches after a local smoke run.

The initial raw+split 90-session seed may exceed the 1,000-request/day ceiling with bounded pages and must checkpoint across days. Routine runs reuse immutable history and fetch missing sessions; changed actions trigger adjusted-history reacquisition. Cached mapping expiry, initial backfills, mass corporate actions or many removed members may exhaust the deadline/budget. Retain partial/unavailable output; do not reduce the universe or bypass limits. Measure actual provider pagination before claiming schedule capacity. Track table/storage growth and retain immutable input tombstones if a later retention policy archives data; no automatic history deletion is implemented.

## Operational gates still open

No paid resources were provisioned. No live deployment, provider capture or before-08:00 observation is claimed by this implementation. The required three observed US report sessions remain open: record start/completion/retrieval timestamps, expected session, symbol coverage/reasons, explicit SIP/basis/action versions, pagination/retries and actual observed availability. Three observations demonstrate operation, not an SLA. The DST/holiday/early-close fixtures do not substitute for live mornings.

Before enabling unattended operation, reconcile real identity/action exceptions, measure full remote D1/Worker CPU/heap/query and combined provider workload, verify current provider buckets and permissions, establish the foundation's private deployment/billing/backup gates, and perform an encrypted restore including the new tables. Never fabricate historical acquisition timestamps to close these gates.

Provider contracts checked during implementation: [Alpaca bars](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockbars), [auctions](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockauctions-1), [actions](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/corporateactions-1), and [OpenFIGI](https://www.openfigi.com/api/documentation). Action availability is not guaranteed at announcement time; a successful response cannot establish universal completeness.
