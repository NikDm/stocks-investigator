# Prospective outcomes

Issue 11 adds local calculation and private comparison views. Open **Prospective evaluation** from the dashboard's retained report, or `/evaluation/<snapshot-id>?horizon=20`. H20 is primary; H10/H30 are supporting. The member API `/api/outcomes/<snapshot-id>` uses the same horizon and a stable `after` cursor, returning at most 100 members. Calculation histories retain at most 100 revisions per page with a sequence cursor.

The saved eligible universe, rule version, calendar and morning/refresh membership own the denominator. Current catalogue, tracking, purchases and later research evidence are absent from the calculation interface. Missing reference, endpoint, action evidence and awaiting-calculation gaps remain visible. Before maturity the result is pending; after close but before the next report deadline, collection is still open. No price is carried forward and no endpoint is moved.

Session S is one. Outcomes use verified primary-venue Q auction references and raw provider daily closes. Gross entitled dividends accrue as receivables with no reinvestment or tax; splits, cash/stock/mixed conversions, spin-offs and verified worthless cancellations use explicit entitlements. A cancellation may be represented by verified zero cash and no successors; delisting alone cannot establish this. Entry-session effective actions are already reflected in the raw opening buyer's position; that buyer has no entry-session dividend entitlement. Same-day actions require distinct verified ordering. Missing successor endpoint prices are unresolved action gaps. Cash persists without interest. Missing successor prices remain unresolved.

Returns use decimal arithmetic and half-even display values at twelve decimal places. The default 10bp-per-side allowance and 0/25bp sensitivity are hypothetical conventions. Downside is the worst observed daily-close return from the opening reference, before costs. Partial paths report their observed/expected count separately from complete paths; neither daily lows nor stale prices supply missing observations.

## Collection and immutable revisions

Collection runs plan one unplanned frozen snapshot per invocation, including pre-existing saved universes. They queue all H10/H20/H30 members with known endpoint deadlines. Unknown calendar endpoints remain visibly pending; operational calendar repair is required rather than weekday synthesis. The acquire stage calculates up to 240 work rows, in partitions of at most 20 members, with a 15-second between-partition guard. Raw input and auction partitions cap at 2,000 and 1,000 rows; action lookup caps at 500 event identities associated with the holding window, following only those identities across corrected dates through indexed lookups; unusually large/action-bearing histories take a separately bounded path (20 successor identities, 500 observations per security), at most once per tick. Reads page at 100 members; completed initial work leaves the indexed due queue. Raw observations, frozen decisions and revision provenance remain intact.

Initial calculations always use acquisition cutoffs at the next exchange-report 08:00 Zagreb deadline after their endpoint. The opening reference has its own earlier next-report deadline. Running a job later never extends either cutoff. Completed, contiguous retained action-request pages establish queried interval coverage, including empty responses. Provider action rows remain **unverified** until reconciled. Action completeness and late process-date reporting are still provider limitations; a completed query is evidence of what was returned, not proof of unavailable terminal facts.

Operator integration uses `Outcomes.reviewAction(lease, { inputId, reason, evidence, currency: 'USD', timing: 'verified_effective_session', order, entitlement })` against a retained action observation. Evidence must establish the economic date, USD consideration, conversion ratios and any successor identities; uncertain timing/currency/rights remain unresolved. Correct erroneous source dates with a retained market-observation revision before reconciling. Supported entitlements are `split { ratio }`, `dividend { cash }`, `conversion { cash, successors: [{ securityId, ratio }] }`, `spinoff { successors }` and `unresolved`. Successor identity/collection evidence must also be registered through the existing market collection boundary. This internal operator API is fenced and deliberately has no browser form that could imply automatic verification.

After reconciliation or late/corrected data, call `Outcomes.calculate(lease, snapshotId, securityId, horizon, correctionReason)`. A nonempty reason and an existing initial result are required; the correction links its previous revision, cutoff, frozen calculation inputs, hash and provenance identifiers. Initial results and selections are immutable. Observation revisions follow retained acquisition receipts, including a correction that reverts to earlier content. Official auction event identity preserves full timestamp, venue and condition; a later unrelated auction cannot supersede a valid Q. Action revisions use stable provider event IDs across corrected effective dates, so old and corrected dates cannot double-count one entitlement. Ordinary reruns return the existing result, so later evidence cannot silently replace it. Corrections require explicit operator action; new arrivals do not automatically label themselves verified corrections. The UI shows latest revisions and links the full original history.

## Comparison and operational limits

Only certified on-time morning reports enter morning comparisons. Certified complete on-time refreshes remain separate descriptive comparisons with their matched frozen baseline. Empty AI is abstention. Coverage, reason counts and resolved-only mean/median, overlap and matching paired coverage accompany returns; baseline members not selected by AI retain their outcomes. Feedback response/usefulness/time denominators remain independent of returns. Small samples, overlapping cohorts and incomplete coverage cannot establish statistical edge.

The full-universe local harness verifies bounded planning, calculation and pagination for 5,078 retained members. It does not establish hosted throughput with live sources, terminal-action coverage or predictive usefulness. Monitor the age/count of `outcome_work` due rows alongside source budgets and collection progress; growing backlog requires more measured capacity, not shrinking the denominator. Existing deployment/private-access/morning/budget/backup gates remain open. Jobs remain disabled; no paid call, deployment or provisioning is part of this ticket.
