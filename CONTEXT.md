# Investment Intelligence

Personal investment research centered on discovering stock opportunities and following manually selected candidates over time.

## Language

**Discovery**:
The automatic identification of potential investments for manual review, initially focused on momentum and emerging attention in stocks.

**Daily review**:
The investor's once-per-day review of investment candidates and developments affecting selected investments.

**Holding period**:
The intended duration of an investment, initially a few weeks.

**Unexplained momentum**:
Observed price strength for which the research has not identified a supporting explanation. It is an explicit information gap, not evidence that no explanation exists.

**Early watch**:
A discovery category for stocks showing emerging attention without market confirmation. Inclusion does not establish an actionable momentum opportunity.

**Tracked opportunity**:
A candidate the investor manually selects for ongoing assessment. Tracking does not imply ownership.

**Recorded purchase**:
An optional record of an actual stock purchase, including its entry date and price, distinct from selecting an opportunity to track.

**Opportunity thesis**:
The recorded reasoning for why an opportunity's momentum might continue over an expected holding period, including evidence that would invalidate that reasoning.

**Assessment**:
An explicit judgment of an opportunity against its recorded thesis, supported by available evidence and expressed uncertainty. Insufficient evidence is a valid assessment outcome.

**Assessment version**:
An immutable published assessment with its information cutoff, cited evidence, and market-data timestamps. A later assessment supplements rather than replaces the historical record.
