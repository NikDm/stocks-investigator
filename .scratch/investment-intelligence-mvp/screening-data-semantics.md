## Daily observations

Alpaca groups daily trades by New York date; the timestamp marks the interval start, not publication time. Trade conditions and tape determine which fields update. Extended-hours conditions `T`/`U` contribute daily volume without updating daily prices. Thus daily volume is not regular-session-only volume, and summing regular-session minute volumes changes the metric. `Q` means market-center official open and does not update bar prices; `O` means opening trade and can update them. Multiple conditions use the strictest rule. No bar is emitted when qualifying OHLC/volume fields are absent. Consequently missing observations cannot become zero, and a daily or minute open is not proof of an official primary-market open. [Alpaca aggregation rules](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/market-data-faq#how-are-bars-aggregated).

Historical bars support explicit `feed=sip` (all US exchanges), `adjustment`, and dated symbol resolution via `asof`. `split` adjusts prices and volumes for forward/reverse splits; `dividend` adjusts prices for cash dividends; `spin-off` adjusts prices; `all` combines these; `raw` applies none. `asof` identifies the symbol's underlying entity and resolves earlier names; it is **not documented as an adjustment-information cutoff**. Start/end bounds are inclusive. Limits apply across symbols; exhaust page tokens. [Historical bars reference](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockbars).

Design constraint: name any screening volume metric as provider daily consolidated eligible volume if using `1Day.v`; use one consistent adjustment basis across numerator and history. Do not describe this as regular-hours volume. Split-adjusted prices/volumes are a practical basis for comparable screening history; raw auction references require consistent split/action arithmetic when compared with later prices. Save fetched inputs and retrieval time: rerunning a dated query alone does not establish a frozen historical dataset or prevent revised adjustments from changing results.

## Opening reference

Auctions are SIP-only. Historical requests accept explicit bounds, `asof`, currency and pagination; no adjustment parameter is documented. Do not assume auction prices have received the bar endpoint's split/dividend adjustments. [Historical auctions reference](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockauctions-1).

Saved November 2 evidence includes opening arrays for all five sampled symbols. Each event carries `p`, `s`, `t`, `x`, `c`. AAPL/MSFT each have an earlier NYSE Arca (`P`) official-open event than their Nasdaq (`Q` exchange code) events. STN likewise has another venue opening before NYSE (`N`). UFI has another venue event at 09:40 ET. The sample therefore directly disproves choosing the first returned event as a listing-market opening rule. Here `Q` as an **exchange** is Nasdaq, while `Q` as a **condition** denotes official open; retain both fields distinctly. [Saved requests, timestamps and exchange-code response](market-semantics-check.json).

Reproducible proposed rule for ticket 02: resolve and freeze the dated primary-listing venue, map it to the provider exchange code, and select that venue's `Q` official-open event for the target exchange session. Keep its original event timestamp; an opening may be delayed. A duplicate with identical venue/time/price may be deduplicated; conflicting qualifying prices remain unresolved pending a defined correction policy. Set a declared collection cutoff, preserve retrieved evidence and retrieval timestamps, and record missing/unknown-venue/conflicting references explicitly. Do not silently substitute another venue, an `O` event, daily open, or minute open. Any permitted proxy needs a separate named methodology and status.

This selection rule is a proposed product policy, not an Alpaca guarantee of unique events, finality, universal coverage, executable fills, or a specific deadline. The five-symbol sample does not resolve those limits. A dated venue mapping must cover NYSE/Nasdaq/NYSE American/Cboe/TXSE cases or visibly exclude unresolved mappings; broker schedule labels alone are insufficient according to the existing coverage check.
