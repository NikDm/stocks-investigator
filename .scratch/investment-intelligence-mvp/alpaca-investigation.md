Do not infer that a funded brokerage account or paid subscription is necessary, or that a new paper account is sufficient. The [bars reference](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockbars) accepts comma-separated symbols and up to 10,000 **total** bars/page, default 1,000; pages can be shorter. Results sort by symbol then timestamp: drain `next_page_token` before declaring completeness. Adjustments include raw, split (price/volume), dividend (price), spin-off (price), and all. `asof` resolves the symbol's entity/name history; it is not an as-published data vintage. Feed choices include SIP, IEX, BOATS and OTC. Dates are inclusive. Handle HTTP 429 using the rate-limit headers. The reference does not state a maximum symbols-per-request; a 100-symbol batch is a planning choice, not an asserted provider limit.

## What price/volume fields mean

The [FAQ aggregation table](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/market-data-faq) defines daily intervals by New York date, not a strict 09:30–16:00 timestamp filter. Extended-hours condition `T` updates daily volume but not daily OHLC; it updates minute OHLC/volume. Official-open condition `Q` updates neither prices nor volume; opening-trade condition `O` can update both. Thus daily `open` is not a guaranteed primary-exchange official opening auction, and daily volume is not strictly regular-session volume. Rename mapping may lag until the following day. OTC data requires a special subscription currently offered only to Broker partners.

Implication for ticket 02: choose whether daily consolidated volume including eligible extended-hours prints is the desired screening measure. If regular-session volume is required, daily `v` is unsuitable without a different calculation/data contract. Keep volume definitions consistent across history and signals. For evaluation, agree an explicitly regular-session reference (for example a qualifying consolidated trade after 09:30 ET, or a bounded regular-session minute bar retrieved later). That proposed proxy must not be described as an official auction price; a minute bar is not the investor's executable fill.

[Real-time stock-data documentation](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/real-time-stock-pricing-data) explicitly includes premarket/aftermarket minute bars and describes late-trade updated bars. This supports the existence of extended-session observations; access to historical SIP still depends on the account check above. Historical queries ending before now minus 15 minutes, with clock margin, are the proposed mechanism. Save event time, interval, retrieval time, feed and delay; a missing interval is unavailable, not a zero price. At 08:00 Zagreb the ordinary 04:00 ET premarket has not begun (New York is ordinarily 02:00, or 03:00 during DST mismatch); morning reports use the prior completed session. Overnight BOATS is a separate feed and is not substituted for SIP premarket.

No reviewed source promises a finalized previous-session delivery time before the morning deadline. Corrections can change later observations. Compare results before 08:00 Zagreb with a later retrieval during an authorized test, then preserve immutable assessment inputs rather than replacing history silently.

## Actions, delistings and calendar

The [corporate-actions endpoint](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/corporateactions-1) supports splits, dividends, spin-offs, mergers, name changes, worthless removals, reorganizations and other action types. Results use `process_date` boundaries, default to today, paginate at up to 1,000 records, and default `data_quality=complete` excludes unprocessed incomplete records; `all` includes early incomplete records. Alpaca explicitly gives no creation-time guarantee: provider/processing delays can make announced actions absent initially. Poll overlapping process-date ranges and preserve revisions as a proposed operational measure; absence is not proof of no action.

No reviewed documentation guarantees complete delisted histories, merger consideration or terminal returns for every broker symbol. Free SIP is particularly insufficient to assume ongoing marks after an exchange delisting into OTC, given the FAQ's OTC entitlement restriction above. A bounded test must include renamed, inactive, split, merged, halted and OTC-transition cases. Persist removed identities and label unresolved outcomes; do not discard them or invent a zero terminal value.

The [calendar endpoint](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/legacycalendar) documents market days from 1970 through 2029, with open/close times including early closes. It is under the Trading API (`paper-api.alpaca.markets/v2/calendar` in the reference), so test access separately from market-data credentials. Refresh its finite future horizon and account for exceptional closures. Use America/New_York sessions and Europe/Zagreb report scheduling.

## Agreements: explicit clauses and unresolved interpretations

The [Alpaca disclosure library](https://alpaca.markets/disclosures) links the current customer agreement, general terms, Nasdaq subscriber agreement and NYSE display-services agreement. The two Alpaca-hosted exchange PDFs returned HTTP 403 in this research browser. Their exact incorporated versions were therefore **not** inspected successfully. Official exchange forms below are useful primary-source context, not proof of the investor's accepted text.

The [general terms](https://files.alpaca.markets/disclosures/library/TermsAndConditions.pdf), “Personal and Non-Commercial Usage,” allow personal/noncommercial purposes; making an application available to others or other uses triggers 30 days' written notice. They do not say a private single-user application requires notice merely because it runs in the cloud. The data-plan section explicitly incorporates exchange agreements for paid Pro where applicable; brokerage relationships have additional terms. No examined clause supplies a raw-data retention period or an LLM-specific exception.

The [current customer agreement](https://files.alpaca.markets/disclosures/library/AcctAppMarginAndCustAgmt.pdf), section 30, says: “I agree not to reproduce, distribute, sell or commercially exploit the market data in any manner without written consent from Alpaca.” It also incorporates exchange subscriber agreements where applicable and disclaims timeliness/completeness guarantees. This is a concrete restriction, broader than a commercial-redistribution-only summary. Whether private persistent snapshots/backups constitute restricted reproduction, or are covered by another account-specific authorization, is unresolved; the sentence alone does not define incidental caching or declare all calculation forbidden. Check applicability to the actual account; a brokerage agreement should not automatically be imposed on a Paper Only relationship without evidence.

The official [NYSE Exhibit B](https://www.nyse.com/publicdocs/nyse/data/Exhibit_B_Nonprofessional_and_Metered_Usage_Addendum_Electronic.pdf), paragraphs 1, 5 and 9–11, treats information derived from market data as market data, forbids furnishing it to another person/entity, and limits nonprofessional receipt to personal, nonbusiness use after vendor qualification. It provides no explicit fixed retention duration. A derived momentum score is consequently not automatically outside the restrictions. This form is dated March 4, 2013; confirm the Alpaca/account version before relying on it.

The official [Nasdaq online subscriber agreement package](https://nasdaqtrader.com/content/AdministrationSupport/AgreementsData/subagreemonlappl.pdf), section 1, restricts furnishing or providing others access, including another office/place, and licenses nonprofessional/private use personally. It refers expressly to receipt, processing, display and redistribution, so the text is not evidence of a blanket calculation ban. Section 2 permits subsequent use only under agreement/Nasdaq requirements; this is not unconditional perpetual archival permission. Securities professionals/business use do not qualify as nonprofessional solely because a dashboard is private. Confirm actual subscriber classification and accepted version.

| Proposed use | Evidence-based disposition |
