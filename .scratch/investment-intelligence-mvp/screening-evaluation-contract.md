# Screening and evaluation contract — v1

Resolved research defaults for [issue 02](issues/02-screening-and-evaluation-contract.md), dated 2026-11-03. This is a domain contract for ticket 03 to refine into implementation tasks, not application implementation or evidence of investment edge. Product boundaries remain those in [discovery](../../docs/discovery.md) and [spec](spec.md).

## Authority, evidence and versions

Use rule version `screen-eval-v1` prospectively from the first operational run. Numeric defaults below are design choices, not empirically optimized thresholds or personal risk limits. No prospective outcomes were used to select them. Any change to thresholds, ranking, reference, source/session semantics or missing-data arithmetic creates a new version effective for future runs. Never recompute old selections under new rules. Data corrections append a calculation revision with old/new values, reason, acquisition time and provenance; preserve the original decision and evidence cutoff.

The [full-universe audit](coverage-check.md) establishes practical historical-SIP, auction and action access, including exceptions; [source/cost evidence](public-research-check.md) establishes budget assumptions. Neither establishes universal coverage, a morning SLA or complete terminal outcomes. [Official bars documentation](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockbars), [aggregation rules](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/market-data-faq), [auctions](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockauctions-1) and [corporate actions](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/corporateactions-1) own provider semantics. Provider-defined daily fields include extended-session eligible activity: v1 deliberately uses those consistently, rather than calling them regular-session OHLCV. The evaluation entry reference below is separate. See the accompanying [semantics source check](screening-data-semantics.md).

Store each run's rule version, calendar/session identifiers, start/completion/publication times, information cutoff, acquisition times, broker catalogue/mapping versions, provider/feed/adjustment/as-of parameters, immutable input identifiers/hashes, per-security eligibility/exclusion reasons, metrics/ranks, research queue and decisions, source/evidence identifiers, both shortlists and cohort freezes. Distinguish source publication/event time from first acquisition. Evidence is usable for a decision only if acquired by its cutoff and published by that cutoff; unknown publication time is labeled and cannot establish a time-sensitive claim. Retain permitted source material or a traceable factual note/URL/hash with an explicit replay gap. Do not assume search-snippet archival rights.

## Company shares and dated broker identity

Start with every broker `STOCK` whose joined working schedule is US NYSE/NASDAQ and currency USD, as in the audit; suffixes do not define membership. Resolve dated broker ticker + ISIN + schedule to a unique US composite/share-class FIGI and dated provider symbol. Retain provider asset UUID where present, primary listing venue, security type, issuer identity and reconciliation evidence. A provider asset is not required when verified identity/listing and actual bars exist (MLEC/INDV demonstrate why); broker availability is required. Broker catalogue presence means metadata availability, not a guaranteed account order. Show account availability as unverified unless account-specific evidence exists; require investor confirmation in Trading 212 before acting.

Include ordinary/common company shares, ADRs representing company equity, equity REIT shares and NY registered company shares. Exclude ETFs, closed-end funds, units, warrants, preferred/debt instruments, royalty trusts, MLPs and limited-partnership interests. This operationalizes company shares, rather than expanding into packaged funds or partnership units. Label ADR/REIT type and their structural uncertainties; do not infer issuer domicile from US listing. Each share class remains a separate security, but at most one security per issuer enters either shortlist or early watch; choose its highest deterministic rank. Unknown issuer grouping is quarantined from selection pending reconciliation.

Require verified current US exchange listing and no contradictory identity evidence. Reconcile provider venues NYSE/NASDAQ/AMEX/BATS/TXSE against actual primary listing, rather than treating the broker schedule as the primary venue. Ambiguous ISIN mappings, unlisted-only FIGI matches, conflicting type/listing metadata and unidentified records remain in the exception ledger until resolved; never fuzzy-match names or strip suffixes to accept them. Maintain dated rename/action links; a recycled symbol is not the same security. Refresh broker availability each report; an unavailable or unresolved security cannot become a new selection, but existing histories and outcomes survive removal.

## Data-ready eligible universe and sustained momentum

Let `D` be the last completed US exchange session before the report. Use an exchange calendar with holidays, DST and early closes; never count weekdays as sessions. The eligibility ledger retains all broker inputs. `U_D`, the saved data-ready eligible universe, contains all identity/type/listing-qualified securities satisfying the following quality/liquidity rules, whether or not they pass momentum. Save the broader qualified-but-missing set separately so coverage omissions remain visible.

Use explicit historical `sip`, dated symbol resolution, and split-adjusted daily bars as acquired by the run cutoff. Dividend adjustments are excluded from screening; outcome distributions use the action ledger below. Keep raw latest prices for the $5 test and raw price-times-volume for liquidity. Require all 61 consecutive expected sessions `D-60…D`: valid positive OHLC, positive volume, consistent low/high bounds, no duplicate conflicting sessions and no unresolved action affecting comparability. Never forward-fill or treat a missing bar as zero. A newly listed share with fewer than 61 sessions waits; the 90-session feasibility seed supports this rule without requiring minute histories. Unknown action comparability is a data gap, not a momentum signal.

Defaults:

| Measure | v1 rule |
| --- | --- |
| Price | Latest raw daily close at least USD 5 |
| Liquidity | Median of raw close × raw volume over `D-19…D` at least USD 5 million/day |
| Returns | `R_k = C_D / C_(D-k) - 1`, for k = 5, 20, 60; C is split-adjusted daily close |
| Trend | `R_20 ≥ 5%`, `R_60 ≥ 10%`, `R_5 > 0`; `C_D > SMA20 > SMA60`; moving averages include D |
| Persistence | At least 12 positive close-to-close returns among the last 20; largest positive daily log return / sum of positive daily log returns over those 20 at most 0.50 |
| Relative strength | `RS20 = R20 - median(R20 of U_D)` strictly positive; compute median before momentum filtering |
| Volume confirmation | Mean split-adjusted volume `D-4…D` / mean volume `D-24…D-5` at least 1.20 |

Use decimal arithmetic with at least 12 decimal places for ratios, round half-even at 12 places before threshold comparisons/ranking, and display fewer digits without reranking. Dollar volume uses unadjusted contemporaneous prices and volumes; applying today's split factor to price alone would corrupt it. If split-adjusted volume semantics cannot be verified for a run, withhold affected metrics rather than mixing bases. Positive-day count excludes flat sessions; zero total positive log return fails persistence. Volume refers to provider daily eligible activity including extended sessions, not regular-hours-only trading.

These coarse defaults reject sub-$5 and thin activity, require three months of comparable observations and multiweek strength, reduce dependence on a single jump, and fit the verified 90-session seed. They are neither stop-loss rules nor suitability judgments. Relative strength uses the saved broker universe median to avoid a new benchmark-data dependency; it is not sector/market-risk adjustment. Early-close sessions count normally, with no invented volume annualization. The rolling ratio can be distorted by early closes and event volume; show this limitation.

A run is `complete` only when every in-scope broker input is resolved into an eligibility/exclusion decision with required data; unresolved identity/history/provider gaps produce `partial`, with counts and reasons. Structural exclusions and explicitly known new-listing history insufficiency are decisions, not transport failures. A known history gap still prevents that security entering U_D. If the expected session/calendar/catalogue or required inputs for any shortlist cannot be established, publish `unavailable` and no new selections. A partial run may publish evaluated candidates from U_D, visibly labeled partial; freeze its exact denominator. Never reuse yesterday's report as current. If U_D is empty, return empty; no median or ranking is invented.

## Ranking, bounded research and final candidates

Among momentum passers sort descending by RS20, then R60, volume ratio, median dollar volume, then ascending stable security identity (composite FIGI + share-class FIGI + ISIN). Use one representative per issuer as above. This is the screener-only ordering; preserve full ranks and reasons, including qualified securities outside the bounded research queue.

The baseline shortlist is the first three issuer-distinct passers, or fewer when fewer qualify. Research at most three discovery securities per scheduled day: the same first three in that order. This matches issue 01's three-candidate budget and creates an auditable equal-input comparison; v1 assesses the value of filtering/interpretation, not unconstrained AI stock search. No replacement candidate is researched when one is rejected. AI can select any subset of those three, ordered: supported thesis, unexplained momentum with explicit conditional thesis, then original screener rank within each group. Contradictory or insufficient evidence withholds a positive main selection and leaves the rejection visible in the candidate's research result. Supporting evidence requires a cited fact and a reasoned connection, not a claimed proven cause. Early watch is a separate labeled list, never a fourth main candidate. Fewer than three, including zero, is valid.

A repeated top-ranked security remains in the baseline and research queue; cached evidence plus change review can satisfy the bounded pass, with actual work logged. Do not skip repeats to manufacture novelty. Mark `new` if never previously surfaced in main; `repeat` otherwise, including prior main date and assessment changes. Early-watch-to-main first appearance is `promoted/new`. Unchanged repeats may be visually compact, but their frozen cohort membership remains. Tracking cards and main selection are separate: a tracked stock can qualify again and is labeled as such, with duplicate evidence work shared. Hard research/search/token caps and retries are refined by 03; budget failure records an unresearched gap and cannot be portrayed as an evidence rejection.

## Compact early watch

Capacity is five issuer-distinct securities, including retained entries. Eligible intake must be in U_D, fail at least one momentum gate, and have either (a) a new issuer announcement/filing with a concrete plausibly material event, or (b) two independent editorial origins about a concrete event within the last three exchange sessions. An issuer release plus copies/syndication of it is one origin; two URLs or publishers quoting the same wire/report are one. Record originating event, origin identifiers, publication/acquisition time, and the reason emerging attention is plausible. This is a qualitative, cited intake rule; no claim of exhaustive attention measurement is made.

Use the existing three bounded broad searches/day and filings/issuer change intake. Rank acceptable intake by primary-source-backed event first, then independent-origin count capped at three, latest originating publication time, then stable security identity ascending. Retained entries compete under the same ordering; no reserved slots. Research only short event notes within existing broad-intake retrieval budgets, not five additional full assessments. If identity, event or independence cannot be established, do not admit. Conflicting evidence is shown; unsupported positive interpretations are withheld.

An entry expires after five exchange sessions counting its first appearance as session one, unless a distinct new qualifying event restarts that clock; syndicated repetition never restarts it. Remove on lost eligibility, disproven event or promotion, preserving history. Promotion requires every momentum gate and a position in the normal top-three research queue; early watch cannot bypass ranking, market confirmation or research capacity. A confirmed stock outside that queue leaves early watch as `confirmed, not selected`; store that reason. Event evidence can support the normal research result once selected. No Gmail/Reddit intake or continuous monitoring is introduced.

## Material changes, thesis expiry and active tracking

Each thesis records supporting facts/unknowns, expected holding period, explicit independently checkable invalidation predicates, any price-condition method and assessment expiry. Default assessment expiry is the next scheduled report's 08:00 Europe/Zagreb deadline; expire sooner at a known relevant event/opening if the assessment depended on a pre-event/pre-opening condition. Passing expiry means stale assessment, not falsified thesis or archive. A delayed manual re-review cannot validate current entry conditions.

On each scheduled review, flag a material market change when any occurs: absolute split-adjusted daily close return at least 5%; R20 changes by at least 5 percentage points since the last published assessment; a momentum gate changes pass/fail; latest adjusted daily volume / preceding 20-session mean at least 2; or a recorded, method-supported thesis price predicate is crossed using comparable daily observations. Evaluate close predicates at close; a daily low can only establish an observed low touch, not its sequence or an executable stop. Premarket re-review may flag an absolute delayed observed price change of at least 5% versus previous comparable close, labeled interval/delay and non-executable. Missing data is a separate material quality deterioration, never a zero price move.

Material evidence changes include a new earnings/guidance announcement or revision, regulatory/legal decision, financing/dilution, merger/delisting/action, retraction/correction, or sourced contradiction/resolution of a recorded thesis assumption. Link the changed fact and origin to the thesis; repeated coverage of the same fact is not a change. Market strengthening does not override a contradictory fact. Version the assessment when a change is material; otherwise publish a dated no-material-change check with evidence cutoff, without pretending the old assessment is newly researched. An expired assessment needs a new published assessment to become current.

Invalidation ends the positive thesis immediately upon observed confirmation of a recorded predicate; preserve its record and mark the latest assessment invalidated. Tracking remains active until its existing checkpoint, providing material-change reviews and possible revised reasoning, with no automatic positive thesis replacement. Unknown evidence produces insufficient evidence, not a fabricated invalidation. A replacement thesis requires a new version and explicit reasoning; it does not reset tracking age automatically.

Tracking selection starts a 20-session checkpoint: first exchange session on/after selection is session one, session 20's regular close is the checkpoint. Mark `needs renewal` then; pause active research until manual renewal, retain history and fixed-horizon evaluations. Needs-renewal records occupy a slot in the ten-record capacity until archived or renewed; renewal sets a new 20-session interval starting the next exchange session and requires a current assessment. Invalidation and assessment expiry do not automatically free slots or stop evaluation. None of these defaults is a personal loss tolerance or automated trading instruction.

## Opening reference and frozen cohorts

Each morning report published by 08:00 Zagreb for session S freezes its morning selection/evidence at publication. Both AI and baseline use the identical U_D, market snapshot and freeze, with the same-day upcoming US regular opening S. A late report remains available as late research but is excluded from the on-time morning cohort; never backdate it. Schedule/manual clocks use exchange time `America/New_York`, not a fixed UTC offset.

The absolute pre-opening freeze is scheduled regular open minus five minutes (normally 09:25 ET). A manual re-review must finish strictly before that cutoff. Freeze a separate `refresh` cohort per successful re-review, referring to the same morning universe, market metrics, candidate queue and baseline membership; only evidence/AI decisions change. Re-review scope is the morning three research candidates plus up to ten active tracked opportunities, deduplicated. Tracking-only names do not enter the evaluated discovery shortlist. Multiple refreshes remain separate descriptive cohorts; the latest is never substituted for morning, and do not pool them into the primary comparison. Late/incomplete refreshes are research-only, with no opening-based cohort. Baseline membership is unchanged but gets a matching refresh cutoff record; no later market knowledge reranks it.

Entry reference: the raw `p` of a primary-listing-venue official opening auction from the SIP auctions endpoint for S. Map primary venues explicitly: NYSE→N, NASDAQ→Q, AMEX/NYSE American→A, BATS/Cboe BZX→Z, TXSE→F, using the saved provider exchange-code response and verified listing metadata. Unknown/contradictory venue is reference-unavailable, never inferred from working schedule. Select condition `Q` (market-center official open) on that venue, event timestamp in [scheduled open, scheduled open + 30 minutes]. Identical duplicate records collapse; if multiple Q records disagree on price, mark conflict pending a sourced correction rather than pick first/last. Multiple consistent Q prices select earliest timestamp and retain duplicates' provenance. Condition `O` is not a fallback; nor are off-primary Q, daily open, first trade or minute-bar open. A delayed official opening within the window qualifies with timestamp; beyond it or absent means missing reference. There is no proxy substitution within v1.

Retrieve after the historical-SIP delay permits, retaining acquisition time, pagination and conflict status. Initial reference collection closes at 08:00 Zagreb on the next exchange-report day after S; mark unresolved then if no qualifying reference was acquired. Initial endpoint/action calculations likewise use inputs acquired by the next exchange-report deadline after the endpoint. Later arrivals or provider corrections append revisions, never silently replace the initial result. The entry reference is observed after the decision; it must never influence selection. Saved universes require whole-universe reference capture, not just selected candidates: issue 01 budgets roughly 51 additional 100-symbol requests/day before retries. Unknown primary mappings or unavailable auctions leave explicit evaluation gaps without changing screening membership.

## Hypothetical fixed-horizon outcomes and downside

All reference returns are hypothetical comparison observations, not executable fills, realized purchase returns or recommendations. Session S is holding session one. H=10/20/30 ends at the provider-defined daily close of exchange session S+(H-1); 20 is primary. Holidays do not count, early closes do. Do not roll endpoints to a later available stock bar. Use the same reference, calendar, costs and endpoint rules for every selected/not-surfaced security. These daily closes can include eligible extended-hours activity; label them `provider daily close`, never official regular-session closing auction. A future regular-close variant needs a new version and coverage evidence.

Start with one share at raw opening reference P0. Apply verified actions in chronological effective order to its share quantities; include gross cash dividend entitlement for ex-dates strictly after entry session and on/before endpoint, without reinvestment or tax assumptions. An ex-date equal to S does not entitle the opening buyer. Accrue entitled cash even if payable later, labeling receivables. Endpoint wealth W_H is sum of resulting shares × their raw daily closes plus cash/receivables. Gross return is `W_H/P0 - 1`. Cost-adjusted return is `W_H*(1-0.001)/(P0*(1+0.001)) - 1`: illustrative 10 basis points each side, identical across cohorts. Show gross and adjusted, plus 0/25-bp-per-side sensitivity; the allowance is a comparison convention, not a measured spread, broker fee or guarantee. No FX, tax, funding or portfolio weighting is implied.

Splits scale quantity, not wealth; avoid double adjustment by using raw outcome prices. Cash merger replaces shares with verified entitlement at its effective date and holds cash without interest to the horizon. Stock/mixed merger follows verified conversion ratio and successor security plus cash; spin-offs follow all distributed securities. Unsupported rights, uncertain consideration/currency, missing successor prices or unresolved action timing produce `unresolved action`, not an assumed value. Verified worthless cancellation can yield zero; delisting alone cannot. Renames preserve security identity. No OTC continuation is presumed available. Removed or merged securities stay in their original cohort denominator.

Interim downside is `min(0, min(W_j/P0 - 1))` over available daily-close wealth observations from S through endpoint, before costs, with action/cash treatment identical to endpoint wealth. Report this as **worst observed daily-close return from reference**, not intraday maximum adverse excursion or peak-to-trough drawdown. Daily lows include pre-entry/extended activity and cannot supply a consistent post-entry path; v1 does not use them to invent intraday downside. If any required close/action is missing, the complete-horizon downside is unavailable; optionally show `partial observed downside` with observed/expected session count, explicitly not comparable to complete paths. Never carry forward stale prices. Terminal verified cash holdings provide cash wealth on later sessions; unknown delisting does not.

Before horizon maturity label pending. After maturity, missing entry/endpoint/action yields an explicit unresolved outcome with reason. Keep all frozen membership counts and report resolved count/total per horizon, missing-reference/endpoint/action counts, medians and arithmetic means for resolved members only. Never rank missing outcomes as zero or silently drop them. Main comparison reports AI and baseline coverage separately and paired results for securities having both required observations, with overlap and rejection outcomes; empty AI selection is abstention, not zero-return cash. Overlapping daily cohorts and tiny samples preclude strong independence/significance claims. Show coverage bias; incomplete cohorts cannot establish outperformance.

## Not-surfaced review and usefulness

For each saved morning U_D, rank resolved hypothetical H20 cost-adjusted returns descending, stable security identity ascending for ties; show top ten, denominator and unresolved count. H10/H30 are supporting views, not alternate optimized winner windows. Exclude no members based on present-day broker status. Mark each as AI-main, baseline-only, early-watch-only, researched/rejected, outside research cap, or momentum-gate failure using its actual frozen records; `not surfaced` means absent from morning AI main. Allow multiple provenance flags (e.g. baseline and rejected). Refresh membership is a separate flag. Eligibility/mapping/history exclusions appear in a separate coverage ledger, never retroactively inserted into U_D. This review cannot establish detectability or causation; evidence first acquired later is labeled hindsight.

At the end of each daily review optionally record elapsed minutes, usefulness (useful / mixed / not useful), and whether it added understanding, saved effort or changed a research decision; allow a short note and explicit no-response. Monthly display response count/eligible reports, median review minutes, proportion within 15 minutes and usefulness distribution, independently of returns. Initial descriptive target is median 10–15 minutes and a majority useful among responses; no-response is not satisfaction. Missed targets prompt review, not threshold fitting on future returns.

## Worked acceptance examples

1. **Identity/coverage:** ELF_EQ_US with a verified US schedule can qualify despite its suffix. An OTC `_US_EQ` does not. An unlisted-only FIGI with no current listing is quarantined; MLEC with independently reconciled identity and history can qualify without a provider trading asset. A 60-session IPO or a 61-session series missing D-7 fails history; neither is forward-filled.
2. **Sustained signal:** adjusted C(D-60)=20, C(D-20)=22, C(D-5)=23, C(D)=25 gives R60=25%, R20=13.6363636364%, R5=8.6956521739%. With U_D median R20=4%, RS20=9.6363636364 percentage points. SMA20=23.5>SMA60=22, 13 positive days, largest positive log gain 0.03 of total positive 0.20 (15%), median dollar volume $8m and 5/preceding-20 volume ratio 1.30 pass. The same returns with a 70% positive-log concentration fail. Ties in RS20 resolve by R60, then the specified remaining keys, never model preference.
3. **Bounds and repeats:** passers A,B,C,D produce baseline A,B,C and only A,B,C are researched. Research rejects B on a cited contradiction and cannot assess C within budget: AI main is A, B rejected, C unresearched; D is not backfilled. If A appeared yesterday it is repeat, still a cohort member. Five early-watch places are a cap, not a quota; three copies of one release count as one origin. Early-watch D passing tomorrow's gates promotes only if in tomorrow's top three.
4. **Freeze/auction:** morning published 07:58 Zagreb freezes then. A 09:24 ET refresh is a separate cohort; one completed 09:25 is late. For NASDAQ, an Arca Q at 09:30:00 is ignored; NASDAQ O at 09:30:01 is ignored; NASDAQ Q at 09:30:01.001 is used. Conflicting primary Q prices are unresolved. A sole 10:01 official open is outside the 30-minute window and no minute price substitutes.
5. **Horizon/cost/downside:** S is session one, so H10 ends nine exchange sessions after S. P0=$100, no actions, H20 wealth=$110 gives gross 10% and adjusted `109.89/100.10-1` = 9.7802197802%. Lowest daily-close wealth $94 gives -6% observed downside, even if an intraday low was $89; intraday downside remains unknown. A missing middle session leaves endpoint return computable if actions are verified, but full downside unavailable. A missing endpoint does not shift to the next day.
6. **Actions/denominator:** P0=$100, 2:1 split, endpoint raw close $55 gives 2×55=$110, not $55 or $220. A $1 dividend entitled before a later split adds $1 cash, giving $111 and gross 11%. A verified $105 cash merger holds $105 to H20 (5% gross); a vanished symbol without verified consideration stays unresolved. In a three-member cohort with returns 10%, -5%, unresolved, report mean 2.5% for 2/3 resolved, never 1.667% for three or an undisclosed two-member result.
7. **Lifecycle:** a close-based invalidation is confirmed on selection session six: the thesis is invalidated, active material-change tracking continues through the existing session-20 checkpoint. At that close research pauses as needs-renewal, retaining its slot; manual renewal starts a new interval, while all original H10/H20/H30 outcomes remain scheduled. Yesterday's expired assessment is neither a new thesis nor renewed tracking.
